Does it hurt?

Placement is done under local anaesthetic, so you feel pressure but not pain. Most people find the days afterwards easier than a difficult extraction. Sedation is available if you are anxious.

How long will I be without teeth?

For a single implant, a temporary tooth covers the gap from day one. For a full arch, fixed teeth are usually fitted the same day as surgery. You do not leave with an empty jaw.

I was told I don't have enough bone. Is that final?

Often not. Grafting, sinus lifts, and zygomatic or pterygoid anchorage make treatment possible in jaws that have lost significant bone. A CBCT scan is the only way to know, and it takes minutes.

How much does it cost?

As a guide: a single implant with its crown runs ₹35,000–₹65,000. A full arch on four implants is ₹3.5–6 lakh per arch. Full-mouth rehabilitation is ₹7–12 lakh. Your written plan carries an exact, itemised figure. Instalments are available above ₹1 lakh.

I'm travelling from another city. Can it be done in fewer visits?

Yes. Out-of-town and overseas patients are usually planned around two trips: surgery with same-day teeth, then the final fit.

I've never placed an implant. Which course should I start with?

Start with Implant Foundations online. It covers case selection through to loading. Pair it with the CBCT reading course, then join a hands-on course to place under supervision.

How long do I have access to online courses?

Self-paced courses come with lifetime access, including future updates to the same course. Live programs run for their stated duration.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. Each course ends with a short assessment, and you receive a certificate of completion with a verification link.

Can I pay from outside India?

Yes. Online courses can be paid in INR or USD by card, UPI or international payment methods.

When is the next hands-on course?

Hands-on courses run several times a year in India and abroad. Join the waitlist or send an enquiry and you will hear about dates first.
