Missing teeth change more than a smile.

Most people wait years before treating them — usually because nobody explained clearly what could be done, how long it takes, or what it costs.

Dentures that never settle

Slipping plates, sore gums, food you have learned to avoid. Removable teeth rest on gum, not bone, so the fit keeps changing.

Bone quietly disappearing

Once a tooth is lost the jawbone under it begins to shrink. The longer the gap stays, the more reconstruction is needed later.

Being told it can't be done

"Not enough bone" is rarely the end of the conversation. Zygomatic, pterygoid and grafting techniques exist for exactly these cases.

An implant is a new root, not a new tooth.

That distinction is why it behaves differently to a bridge or a denture. The titanium post fuses to living bone and takes load the way a natural root does — which is also what stops the bone shrinking.

  • CrownZirconia or ceramic, shade-matched to the teeth beside it.
  • AbutmentThe connector, torqued to a set value so nothing loosens.
  • Titanium rootPlaced to a planned depth and angle from your CBCT scan.
  • JawboneGrows onto the surface over 3–4 months. This is osseointegration.

The best implant is the one the patient forgets is there.

What can be done

Four routes cover almost every case. Which one is right depends on how much healthy bone is left — the scan answers that on your first visit.

01

Single tooth implant

One titanium root, one crown, matched to the teeth beside it. The neighbouring teeth are left untouched — no bridge, nothing filed down.

 3–4 months 02

All-on-4 — teeth in a day

A full arch of fixed teeth supported on four implants, placed and loaded in a single day. You walk out with teeth, not a denture.

 24 hours 03

Zygomatic & complex cases

For severe bone loss where implants have been refused elsewhere. Anchorage is taken from the cheekbone instead of the shrunken upper jaw.

 1–2 days 04

Full-mouth rehabilitation

Rebuilding bite, function and appearance together when years of wear, gum disease or failed work have caught up.

Questions people actually ask

Does it hurt?
Placement is done under local anaesthetic, so you feel pressure but not pain. Most people describe the days afterwards as easier than a difficult extraction. Sedation is available if you are anxious about the procedure.
How long will I be without teeth?
For a single implant, a temporary tooth covers the gap from day one. For a full arch, fixed teeth are usually fitted on the same day as surgery — you do not leave the clinic with an empty jaw.
I was told I don't have enough bone. Is that final?
Often not. Grafting, sinus lifts, and zygomatic or pterygoid anchorage make treatment possible in jaws that have lost significant bone. A CBCT scan is the only way to know, and it takes minutes.
How much does it cost?

As a guide: a single implant with its crown runs ₹35,000–₹65,000 depending on the system used and whether grafting is needed. A full arch on four implants is ₹3.5–6 lakh per arch. Full-mouth rehabilitation is ₹7–12 lakh.

Your written plan after the scan carries an exact figure, itemised, with nothing added later. Instalments through partner finance are available above ₹1 lakh.

How long do implants last?
A well-placed implant in a mouth that is kept clean can last decades. The crown on top wears like anything else and may need replacing sooner. Smoking and uncontrolled diabetes are the two biggest risks to longevity.
I'm travelling from another city. Can it be done in fewer visits?
Yes. Out-of-town and overseas patients are routinely planned around two trips — surgery with same-day teeth, then the final fit. Tell us where you are travelling from and the schedule is built backwards from that.
